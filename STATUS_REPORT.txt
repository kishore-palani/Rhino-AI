ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLES
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Reasoning is separate from execution: AI reasons and tools execute.
2. Modeling skills take priority over raw code generation.
3. Validation runs before and after geometry-changing operations.
4. Failures become structured learning data.
5. The architecture remains model and provider agnostic.
6. Regulations and project context remain explicit and jurisdiction-aware.
